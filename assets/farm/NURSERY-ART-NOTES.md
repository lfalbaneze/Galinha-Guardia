# Abrigo dos pintinhos

Arte original feita para **Penas pro Ar!** com a ferramenta integrada **OpenAI ImageGen**, em 16 de setembro de 2026. O CLI/API não foi usado.

- Imagem selecionada: `assets/farm/chick-nursery.png` (1536 × 1024), preservada sem alteração.
- Referência de estilo: `assets/farm/farm-atlas.png`.
- Cópia offline: `assets/farm/atlas-data.js`.
- O jogo interpreta o fundo branco como transparência; flores brancas e detalhes internos são preservados. A frente baixa do abrigo cobre apenas os pés dos pintinhos.

## Prompt de criação

Create ONE isolated 2D game sprite of a cozy open-front chick nursery shelter for a pixel-art farm adventure. Asset only, transparent background with genuine alpha, no ground island, no scenery, no animals, no people, no lettering or signs. A low WIDE honey-brown wooden shelter, about 2.2 times as wide as tall, under a gently sloped golden thatch roof. Three large open nesting cubbies across its front, each filled with warm straw and with a low wooden lip. Dark warm interiors must be large enough for two small chick characters to be drawn in each cubby by the game. Roof only covers the upper half, nesting openings stay clearly visible. Low sturdy side posts, a few small terracotta flower pots at the outer corners, worn plank details, no steps or ramp. Rich readable 16-bit pixel art with crisp dark pixel outlines, warm sun from top left, elevated three-quarter top-down game view (front and roof visible), non-isometric, no perspective floor grid. Match the warm golden thatch and dark outlined farm building style of the provided reference, but the reference is only style guidance; create only this new wide nesting shelter, not a sheet or a copy of the tall coop. Center the complete object with 5% transparent margin. 1536x1024 landscape.

## Prompt final de edição

Change ONLY the background in this game asset. Replace every black, brown, amber, glowing, shadowy background pixel outside the wooden nesting shelter with FLAT PURE WHITE #FFFFFF. No gradient, no vignette, no glow, no cast shadow, no ground. Keep the shelter, roof, posts, straw, three openings and flower pots intact in their existing positions and colors. White background must extend into all negative space around and beneath the object's outline. The entire upper margin, lower margin and corners must be pure white. Preserve the dimensions and pixel-art edges of the complete object. This is a technical game cutout on white, not a lit scene.
