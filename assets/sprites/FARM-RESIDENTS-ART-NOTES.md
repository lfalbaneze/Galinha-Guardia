# Moradores da fazenda

Registro da primeira versão. Cavalo e peru agora usam as folhas com quatro direções descritas em [CUTE-ART-NOTES.md](CUTE-ART-NOTES.md). A arte e os prompts abaixo permanecem no acervo.

Arte criada com a ferramenta integrada **imagegen** para este projeto. Arquivo consumido pelo jogo: [sources/farm-residents.png](sources/farm-residents.png), 1536 × 1024, com transparência real. Os pixels da folha são preservados; o renderizador apenas recorta, espelha e escala os quadros. Cada linha tem três poses: cavalo castanho, cavalo palomino e peru. A versão atual usa somente cavalo castanho e peru, ambos resgatáveis. Sombras são desenhadas no chão pelo jogo.

Integração atual: `scripts/build-sprite-data.cjs` publica os recortes do cavalo castanho e do peru em `systems/sprite-data.js`. `CharacterArt` desenha ambos como amigos resgatáveis, com três poses laterais e espelhamento à esquerda. Cada espécie aparece uma vez e participa da missão. A linha palomino está preservada no arquivo, mas não é usada. O sistema de moradores decorativos foi removido. A revisão do cenário pode ser refeita com `node scripts/render-farm-life.cjs`.

## Prompt de geração

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ONE production sprite atlas for a top-down cozy farm adventure Canvas 2D game. Generate a 1536x1024 landscape PNG with genuinely transparent alpha background, no ground, no drop shadows, no lettering, no grid lines. EXACTLY a regular 3-column by 3-row atlas of NINE isolated whole-body animal sprites; each sprite fully within its equal 512x341 cell with at least 35 pixels margin from cell edges. All animals face RIGHT in side view with slightly visible back/top to fit a top down RPG world. Row 1: same charming chestnut HORSE with dark mane and tail, short horse ears, white narrow forehead blaze, anatomically natural elegant silhouette, four legs and expressive face in idle, walking stride 1, walking stride 2. Row 2: same horse design and SIZE but cream palomino coat and dark blonde mane in idle, walking stride 1, walking stride 2. Row 3: a bronze farm TURKEY with fan tail, layered feather markings, small blue head and red wattle, yellow beak and two legs, idle, walking stride 1, walking stride 2. Style: beautifully crafted detailed pixel-art game sprites with fine dark warm outlines, soft clusters of pixel shading, warm earthy farm palette, like a premium cozy 16-bit game, organic silhouettes, cute but not baby cartoon. All nine sprites have a consistent top-left light source and a grounded horizontal foot baseline within each cell. Each horse fits about 400px wide and 255px tall; each turkey about 280px wide and 245px tall. Clear gaps between sprites, nothing touches another cell, no cropped ears tails hooves. Transparent empty pixels, NOT a white or colored background, NOT a visible checkerboard. This will be sampled down to 80px horse and 50px turkey sprites in the actual game, so keep silhouettes and features legible.

## Prompt da edição selecionada

Use case: background-extraction. Edit this sprite atlas: remove ONLY the brown backdrop completely to a genuine transparent alpha channel. Every empty pixel around and between animals, including the holes between their legs and between neck/body/tail, must be alpha=0. Preserve all nine animal sprites EXACTLY: same chestnut horses in row 1, palomino horses row 2, turkeys row3, same poses, same coordinates, original 1536x1024 dimensions, exact colors, outlines, pixels, and grid alignment. Do NOT recolor the background white or checkerboard; provide a transparent PNG. Do NOT add shadows. No opaque canvas background. We need cutout sprites ready to overlay on the game's grass.

O arquivo escolhido possui 1.065.845 pixels com alpha menor que 128. Os nove recortes foram verificados com os PNGs reais sobre o cenário, incluindo patas, caudas e margens. As outras variantes de geração não são usadas pelo jogo.
